# PPIndex — Round 2 added (November 2026)

Round 2 is the same questionnaire at **30 different sites**. It is built and reported on its own:
every round 2 site is benchmarked against the round 2 average, never against round 1 numbers.
The combined portfolio card puts both rounds side by side.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-10)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| How round 2 sits next to round 1 | Separate wave. Round 1 cards and numbers are unchanged. |
| Benchmark for a round 2 site's standing | The round 2 sites pooled ("round 2 average"). |
| Which sites get a card | All 30. Sites under 30 responses carry a "small sample" flag. |
| Q19 wording differs between exports | Treated as the same question. |
| Action plans for round 2 | Left out of the cards; generate later in the app (round selector → Site Reports). |
| Portfolio cards | Round 1, round 2, and a combined card. |

## What was found in the data

- 3,007 respondents (2,288 completed), 4,658 comments (4,120 with content on completed responses).
  Identifying columns (IP, network ID, location, browser, OS, date, referring site) are removed
  before anything else reads the file (`data/round2/raw_deid.xlsx`).
- Sites are named by street address in the export; `waves.py` maps them to short names. The two
  Las Vegas sites are told apart by street.
- **Two Service Level items have no answers at all in round 2**: "Ability to get help resolving
  workplace or technology issues while on site" and "Support from a community manager"
  (Importance and Satisfaction both empty). Round 2 Service Level therefore averages six items.
  The cards say so; the combined card compares the rounds on the items both asked.
- Previously an item with no answers would have been labelled "Over Supported" (a comparison
  with an empty value). Items with no answers are now left out. Round 1 has none, so no round 1
  number moves.
- Q19 header text: round 1 "…it is easy to use the PulsePoint as part of my typical workday";
  round 2 "…workplace technology enables me to get my work done effectively and flexibly".
  Treated as the same item per the owner.
- Round 1 site names with a few stray respondents (e.g. Bryn Mawr 30, Lake Success 8) overlap
  with round 2 sites, but none were in round 1 scope, so there is no site with two rounds.

## Round 2 results (completed responses)

- Round 2 average Index **62.3 ± 0.6** (Work Activity 55.0, Physical Feature 60.0, Service Level
  66.5, Digital & Tech 67.6).
- Standing (Index): Above — DC-Washington (n=10), FL-Lake Mary, IA-Urbandale (n=18),
  KY-Louisville, MO-St. Louis, NJ-Iselin. Below — MN-Mendota Heights, NC-Winston-Salem (n=24),
  NY-Latham. The rest are In line. The two small-sample "Above" labels rest on 10 and 18 people.
- Status at the round 2 average (22 items with Importance): 10 Under Supported, 11 Target, 1 Over.
  Highest Friction: focused individual work (34), spaces for focused work (30), spaces for
  discretion (30), tasks requiring discretion (28), thermal comfort (25) — the same top five as
  round 1, at higher Friction.
- Comment sentiment: 56% negative, 30% neutral / mixed, 14% positive. Unclassified share 15.4%
  (round 1: 12.3%) — the keyword rules were tuned on round 1 and have not been checked on
  round 2.

## Round 1 vs round 2 (combined card)

Different sites, so these describe two groups of sites, not change over time. Like-for-like
items; 95% margins from respondent bootstraps stratified by site.

| Measure | Round 1 | Round 2 | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Index | 69.0 | 62.3 | −6.7 ± 0.9 |
| Work Activity | 62.5 | 55.0 | −7.5 ± 1.4 |
| Physical Feature | 66.1 | 60.0 | −6.1 ± 1.0 |
| Service Level (6 shared items) | 75.4 | 66.5 | −8.9 ± 0.9 |
| Digital & Tech | 72.0 | 67.6 | −4.4 ± 0.9 |

All four associate-sentiment questions are lower in round 2 (−0.20 to −0.36 on 1–5). Of the top
comment themes, only Desk Availability & Reservations differs after a Benjamini–Hochberg correction
(18% of round 1 respondents vs 15% of round 2).

## Code

- `waves.py` — wave definitions, round 2 site-name map, small-sample threshold (30).
- `build_data.py --wave round2` builds into `data/round2/`; round 1 (`python build_data.py`) is
  unchanged. Both now also write `item_support.csv` (respondent × item Support, 0–100, no text) and
  record `wave` and `items_not_asked` in `meta.json`.
- `reports.load_context(completers_only, wave)`; benchmark wording comes from the wave; question grid
  splits into tables of at most 15 sites.
- `combined_report.py` — the combined card.
- `action_plan` functions take `plans_dir`; round 2 plans go to `data/round2/action_plans/`.
- App: sidebar "Survey round" selector; combined card downloads on the Site Reports tab; the gold-set
  panel is round 1 only (comment IDs are per round).
- `tests/test_waves.py` — 9 tests. Round 1 site cards render byte-identical to the samples.
